About SolTikz

Built on relationships.
Run on expertise.

SolTikz Consulting is a technology talent and consulting firm working across information technology and healthcare informatics — supporting companies who need specialist people, and professionals who need the right opportunity.

Why SolTikz Talk to our team
Who we are

We work at both ends of the hiring equation.

SolTikz Consulting was founded on a simple observation: the technology hiring market is full of intermediaries, and very few of them genuinely understand either side of the transaction. Employers receive volumes of loosely-matched profiles. Skilled professionals send hundreds of applications into systems that never surface them. Both sides lose time, and the right matches never happen.

We built SolTikz to work differently. On the employer side, we take fewer requirements and work them properly — understanding the stack, the team, the delivery model and the constraints before we source a single profile. On the professional side, we work as a dedicated marketing partner rather than a job board: a named specialist, a resume engineered for each role, daily submissions, and honest feedback at every stage.

Our team brings more than eight years of combined experience in technology marketing and recruitment across US IT services, and our specialisation in healthcare informatics gives us depth in a domain most generalist firms handle badly. Alongside our US operations, our group company StudyDad Overseas Education Pvt. Ltd. in India guides students into technology and health informatics programmes abroad — which means we often support a professional's journey from their first university application through to their consulting career.

The SolTikz Standard

How we work, and what we hold ourselves to.

We represent people accurately.

Every profile we present reflects real, verifiable experience. Our resumes are engineered to communicate capability clearly and to align with a job description — never to manufacture one. It costs us some short-term submissions. It earns us employers who take our calls.

We do fewer things, properly.

We are not a volume desk. A marketing specialist at SolTikz carries a deliberately limited number of profiles so that each one receives real daily attention, real recruiter follow-up, and real preparation before every interview.

We stay involved after the offer.

Placement is a milestone, not an ending. We check in through onboarding, we help with the next contract, and we benchmark rates so our consultants are not left behind by the market.

Mission

To make the connection between technology talent and technology employers faster, fairer and more accurate.

We do it by combining human expertise with tools we build ourselves — so that a good match is not left to luck, volume, or a keyword filter.

Vision

To become the partner of choice for technology and healthcare informatics careers in the United States.

The firm professionals recommend to their friends, and the firm employers call before they post a role.

From the founder

Why we built this the way we did.

SolTikz began with a frustration I saw repeatedly: extremely capable people, with real qualifications and real skills, unable to get past a screening system — and employers on the other side genuinely struggling to find those same people. The gap was not talent. It was representation.

So we built a firm around representation done properly. That means a marketing team that knows the technology well enough to have a real conversation with a hiring manager. It means building our own resume engine rather than reusing a template. It means specialising in healthcare informatics rather than claiming to cover every domain.

And it means being honest with candidates about what their profile can and cannot compete for, even when that is a difficult conversation.

We are a young firm in some of what we do, and I would rather say that plainly than dress it up. In the last six months we have placed 22 professionals. Every one of them was worked individually, by a named person, with full transparency on where their profile went and what came back. That standard is what I intend to protect as we grow.
