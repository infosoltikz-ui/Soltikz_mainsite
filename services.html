01 · Our core service

Full-Time Job Marketing

You have the skills. We make sure the market sees them.

Applying to hundreds of roles alone is exhausting, opaque and — for most people — ineffective. Applicant tracking systems filter on structure and terminology. Recruiters respond to follow-up, not submissions. And knowing which roles are genuinely worth pursuing takes market knowledge that most job seekers have no way to acquire.

SolTikz Full-Time Marketing gives you a dedicated professional whose entire job is getting you interviews. We build your positioning, rewrite your resume for each individual role using our own AI resume tool, apply daily to opportunities that genuinely match your profile, follow up with recruiters, prepare you for every interview, and support you through offer and onboarding.

Full-time placement is all we do. We do not run a bench, we do not split our attention across contract sales, and we do not chase submission volume. One service, done properly.

Selective candidate screening

We don't randomly accept every profile. We screen first, then shortlist.

Most marketing desks take any profile that pays. We don't — and that single decision is why our numbers hold up. Each candidate is assessed against our minimum eligibility and profile requirements, and only shortlisted candidates are onboarded for marketing.

If your profile doesn't qualify today, we'll tell you honestly, explain exactly why, and set out what would close the gap. We would rather give you a real plan than take your money and run applications that were never going to convert. It means we turn work away — and it means every candidate we put in front of an employer is one we can genuinely stand behind.

What we assess

  • Work Authorization status and the timeline attached to it
  • Education and how it maps to real, currently-open roles
  • Technical depth — projects you can defend in a live interview
  • Communication and client-readiness, assessed one-to-one
  • Realistic target roles, locations and compensation expectations
  • Commitment to the process: interviews, prep and follow-through

03 · For employers

IT Staffing & Consulting

Technology talent matched to the requirement — not the keyword.

Hiring technology professionals is rarely a shortage problem. It is a matching problem: too many profiles, too little screening, and too little understanding of what the role actually demands day to day. SolTikz works the other way around. We start with the requirement — the stack, the delivery model, the team structure, the timeline and the constraints — and only then do we source.

Because we screen every candidate before they ever enter our programme, the shortlist you receive has already been filtered twice. Fewer profiles, each one screened for technical capability, communication and environment fit, with availability confirmed before it reaches you.

Technology & Healthcare IT Training

Training that closes the gap between qualified and hireable.

A degree proves you can learn. An interview tests whether you can apply. The distance between the two is where most strong candidates lose offers — and it is closable in weeks, not years, with the right focus.

SolTikz training is built backwards from live job descriptions and real interview feedback. We teach the tools, the terminology and the project narratives that hiring managers in your target roles are actually assessing.

It is also the route back for anyone who does not clear our marketing screening on the first attempt. If the gap is a missing skill or a thin project portfolio, training closes it — and you re-enter screening with something real to show.

  • Hands-on project work you can genuinely speak to in an interview
  • Resume and project-narrative development through ResumeBuilderOne
  • Mock interviews with structured, honest feedback
  • Communication and client-facing readiness
How it connects. Training does not sit in isolation. Professionals who train with us move into screening with a project portfolio and interview readiness already in place — which is precisely why our marketed profiles convert.

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill the recruitment, marketing, or software access services requested.
